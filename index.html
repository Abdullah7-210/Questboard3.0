How Questboard works

Questboard runs in your browser. The standalone HTML version doesn't need an internet connection, and your data is stored locally in that browser.

  • Daily quests: +10 XP the first time you complete them each day. Their checkboxes reset at midnight.
  • One-time quests: +20 XP once. Completed one-time quests stay completed until removed.
  • Levels: every 100 XP earns a new level.
  • Streaks: a day counts when you complete at least 70% of your active quests.
  • Backups: use Export backup before moving devices or clearing browser data. Import restores a downloaded backup.

XP is for motivation, not a measure of your worth. A smaller, realistic quest list is usually better than a perfect-looking list you can't sustain.
